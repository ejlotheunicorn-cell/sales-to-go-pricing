How the suggested price is worked out
  1. Comps: exact model, same brand. Left out: your own listings, lots, parts sold "for" a model, used or for-parts items, and titles mentioning damage (read, scratch, scuff, defect, dent, crack, broken) unless your title says *Read. Active listings from sellers under 98% positive feedback are left out.
  2. Shipping: what the buyer paid for shipping is added to each price, since your listings ship free.
  3. Outliers: when there are 4 or more sold (or active) comps, any more than 20% above or below their median is left out.
  4. Sales: the suggestion is the median sold price, with sales from the last 30 days counted 3×.
  5. Active all higher: if every active listing is above every sale, the suggestion is 2% under the median price of active listings in new condition.
  6. No sales: 2% under the median active price.
  7. Nothing on eBay: 10% under Amazon's new price; if Amazon lists it but isn't selling it, 25% under Amazon's list price (retail).

"Best offer" sales show the asking price, so the real sale was likely lower. The upload file changes the price only; quantity is left blank so your stock counts aren't touched.
